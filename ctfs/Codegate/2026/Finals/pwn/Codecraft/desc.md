In CodeCraft, the server shapes the rules of the world.

You may craft its blocks, structures, and resources however you like. There is only one thing you cannot touch: the stock client.

Build and upload a server pack, then enter your world through the client. Make the client break itself using nothing but the world you crafted.

Craft the world. Break the client.

Portal: https://121.167.147.36
Download: https://codegate2026-final.s3.ap-northeast-2.amazonaws.com/Codecraft.zip

The Portal uses a self-signed TLS certificate. Open it in Chromium with certificate verification disabled:
chromium --ignore-certificate-errors "https://121.167.147.36"

Use this browser instance only for CodeCraft. Select your division, authenticate with your CTFd user token, upload your server pack, and request a session. The client will open in the Portal once the session becomes active.

After connecting to a client session, a cooldown of approximately three minutes will apply before you can request another session.
